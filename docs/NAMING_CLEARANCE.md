## Final clearance checklist

For any HOLD candidate or new candidate, check:
1. Live `.com` registrar
2. `.app`
3. `.io`
4. EUIPO / TMview
5. WIPO Global Brand Database
6. USPTO
7. UKIPO where relevant
8. Apple App Store
9. Google Play
10. Major social handles
11. Company registries / obvious business use
12. Linguistic/translation risks in launch markets

## Launch rule

Do not rename the repository, Appwrite project, Cloudflare service, package names, application metadata, or public brand until a candidate passes final clearance.

After clearance, perform one coordinated rename across brand/domain, packages, Astro sites, Appwrite, Cloudflare services, admin, email, social, legal, SEO and app-store metadata.

## Naming direction

Prefer a genuinely distinctive coined word that works across comics, images, video, audio and mixed media; sounds like consumer entertainment rather than infrastructure software; works internationally; has a strong `.com` candidate; and has no obvious adult-content association.
