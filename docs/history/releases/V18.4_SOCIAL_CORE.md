# V18.4 — Social Core

StoryGalore V1 Social Core is creator/community-centric. It does not attempt to become a generic social network.

## V1 scope

1. Creator profiles
2. Creator following
3. Following feed
4. Creator posts
5. Reactions
6. Comments and replies
7. Mentions
8. Notifications
9. Collections/favorites
10. Sharing
11. Block/mute/report
12. Social moderation
13. 1-to-1 messaging
14. Subscriber-only social content
15. Polls

## Deferred

Public reading lists, creator recommendations, AMA/Q&A, creator milestones, collaborative collections are V1.5 candidates. Friends, stories, short-form video feeds, groups, livestreaming, voice rooms, Discord-like communities, social reputation and large-scale gamification are explicitly deferred.

## Architectural boundaries

Social relationships never grant entitlements. Entitlements remain authoritative for subscriber access. Policy Engine, age/jurisdiction rules, moderation and publication state are evaluated before social exposure. Counters and feeds are rebuildable projections.

## Social graph

`Actor -> follows -> Creator -> publishes -> Post/Publication -> engagement -> Reader -> subscription -> revenue`.

## Realtime

Realtime is delivery only. Authoritative social state is persisted. Clients must reconcile from API state after reconnect or missed events.
