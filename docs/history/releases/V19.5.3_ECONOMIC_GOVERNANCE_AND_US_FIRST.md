# StoryGalore V19.5.3 — Economic Governance & US-First Commercial Readiness

## Canonical launch assumptions

- StoryGalore is operated by a US company.
- The launch commercial market is the United States.
- USD is the launch settlement and pricing currency.
- International commercial execution is disabled until a jurisdiction pack is explicitly registered and human-approved.

These are commercial launch constraints, not permanent domain limitations.

## Closed business risks

V19.5.3 closes the remaining pre-development economic governance gaps identified after V19.5.2:

1. US-first jurisdiction policy.
2. Tax determination boundary.
3. Accounting/ledger boundary between cash, revenue, creator payable, tax and reserves.
4. Refund/chargeback exposure and creator reserves.
5. Revenue Participation Agreements for multi-party rights.
6. Versioned pricing-policy lifecycle.
7. Promotion funding and liability policy.
8. Economic simulation before pricing activation.
9. Economic safety thresholds.
10. Annual plans, gifts, referrals, bundles and premieres as registered commercial products.
11. Future B2B registration without executable launch scope.
12. Additional Admin economic-governance surfaces.

## US tax boundary

The system MUST NOT encode tax rates in application logic. Taxability, nexus, exemptions and applicable rates are resolved by a registered tax policy/provider. Unknown tax conditions block financial finalization rather than becoming zero.

This package does not constitute legal or tax advice. Production tax configuration requires human approval and the appropriate professional/provider validation.

## Accounting boundary

StoryGalore's operational ledger distinguishes:

- cash received
- deferred revenue
- recognized platform revenue
- creator payable
- creator reserves
- tax payable
- processor payable
- refund liability
- chargeback exposure
- infrastructure cost
- contribution margin

This is an operational contract boundary; final statutory accounting remains an explicitly governed downstream responsibility.

## Revenue participation

Multi-party content requires an active Revenue Participation Agreement. StoryPass, bundles, licensing, sponsorship and co-created works cannot allocate revenue when rights ownership or participation shares are unresolved.

## Pricing governance

Commercial policies move through:

`DRAFT → SIMULATED → REVIEWED → APPROVED → SCHEDULED → ACTIVE → RETIRED`

No coding agent, runtime service or Admin UI may silently activate or mutate commercial pricing.

## Economic simulation

The simulator evaluates conservative, base, aggressive, viral, high-media-cost, low-conversion and high-chargeback scenarios. Missing inputs remain UNKNOWN. Simulator results are advisory and cannot mutate production policies.

## Development gate

Development of executable financial behavior may proceed only after:

- all contracts pass syntax validation;
- all required test mappings exist;
- US launch assumptions are accepted;
- tax/accounting boundaries are approved;
- payment/provider contracts are verified;
- economic safety thresholds are populated by approved policy data;
- pricing policies are explicitly activated by human approval.
